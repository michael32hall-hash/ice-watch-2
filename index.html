How the all-stat score works

Each usable performance stat receives equal weight after converting it to a percentile within skaters or within goalies. Those averages form this combined list. Higher is better, except for penalty minutes, goals against, GAA and overtime losses. Games played and ice time are context, not scoring categories. Stats missing for any player in a group, and stats with no variation, are excluded from that group. A high score is a comparison with peers, not a projection or an official NHL rating. Small samples can rank highly.
